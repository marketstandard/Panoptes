Subject: Launch update — new date October 14

Hi team,

I'm going to keep this short. We're moving the mobile banking app launch back by three weeks. The revised launch date is October 14.

The reasons:

- The payment integration failed two of the penetration tests last week, and the fixes need a full retest cycle before we're comfortable shipping.
- App store review flagged our accessibility labels, and the rework touches more screens than we first estimated.
- QA found a data-sync bug that only shows up on older Android devices, which is a big enough slice of our users that we can't ignore it.

I know everyone has been pushing hard, and I don't take an extra three weeks lightly. But a banking app that ships broken is a much worse outcome than a banking app that ships late. Updated milestones will go out in the sprint plan tomorrow morning.

Thank you all for the work you've put in — it shows, and it's exactly why we'll get this right.

Priya
