Subject: Mobile banking app launch delayed — new date October 14

Team,

I am writing to confirm that we are delaying the public launch of the mobile banking app by three weeks. The revised launch date is October 14. This is a hard date for planning, vendor coordination, and customer communications; please update sprint boards, release checklists, and partner notices accordingly.

The delay is driven by the following:

- The KYC vendor will not complete production identity-verification certification until the week of September 22, which blocks a compliant go-live.
- An additional independent security review of session handling and fund-transfer flows was added after the last penetration test, and remediation plus retest will not finish on the original schedule.
- Accessibility QA on iOS and Android uncovered contrast and screen-reader issues in the onboarding and bill-pay screens that must be fixed before we ship.

Engineering, design, compliance, and QA should re-baseline remaining work against October 14 and flag any dependency that cannot move this week. Thank you for the care you have already put into this release and for adjusting quickly so we can launch a product we trust.
