Subject: Mobile banking app launch — revised date

Hi everyone,

I want to share an important update on the mobile banking app launch. After review with engineering and compliance, we are moving the release back by three weeks. The revised launch date is October 14.

The reasons are:

- A late-stage security audit flagged two findings in the biometric login flow that require rework and re-testing before we can ship.
- The third-party KYC vendor has slipped its certification timeline, which gates onboarding for new users.
- We need additional time to complete the accessibility remediations identified in the most recent external review.

I know this is disappointing news after all the hard work that has gone into getting us this far. The program leads will follow up this week with the adjusted sprint plan and a short FAQ for stakeholders.

Thank you all for your patience and for the care you continue to put into this product.
