# Seasoning and Caring for a Cast-Iron Skillet

1. **Strip it down.** If the pan is new, wash off the factory wax or shipping oil with hot water and a little dish soap; if it's an old pan with flaking, sticky buildup or rust, scrub it back to bare gray metal with steel wool. This is the one time a cast-iron pan should ever meet an abrasive that aggressive.

2. **Dry it completely.** Towel the skillet off and then set it over low heat on the stove for a few minutes until every trace of moisture has evaporated. Cast iron will begin to rust within minutes of sitting wet, so dryness is non-negotiable before oil goes on.

3. **Apply a very thin coat of oil.** Rub a neutral high-smoke-point oil — grapeseed, canola, or flaxseed — over the entire pan, inside, outside, and handle included. Then wipe it back off with a clean cloth until the surface looks dry; the invisible film left behind is exactly the right amount.

4. **Bake it upside down.** Place the skillet inverted on the middle rack of a 450–500°F oven with foil or a sheet pan below to catch drips, and bake for one hour. Inverting prevents oil from pooling and turning into sticky spots.

5. **Cool it in the oven.** Turn the oven off and let the pan come down to room temperature inside it. Repeat steps three through five two or three more times for a new or freshly stripped pan — thin layers stacked slowly beat one thick layer every time.

6. **Clean it right after cooking.** While the pan is still warm, rinse with hot water and scrub with a brush, chainmail, or coarse salt; a small amount of dish soap is fine and will not destroy a cured seasoning. Dry it immediately on the stovetop and wipe on a whisper-thin coat of oil before it goes back in the cupboard.

7. **Cook in it often, and know its limits.** Frequent cooking with fat — searing, frying, cornbread — builds the seasoning better than any oven cycle does. Avoid soaking it, running it through the dishwasher, or simmering acidic foods like tomato sauce or wine in it for long stretches until the finish is thoroughly established.
