Subject: Revised Launch Date — Mobile Banking App

Hi team,

I'm writing to let you know that the launch of the mobile banking app will be delayed by three weeks. Our revised launch date is October 14.

The delay comes down to three factors:

- Security review: the external penetration test surfaced two issues that must be remediated and re-tested before we can go live.
- App store approval: the review flagged our biometric login flow, and resolving it requires changes to the onboarding sequence.
- Integration stability: recent load tests showed intermittent timeouts from the core banking API under peak traffic, and we need additional hardening.

I know this is disappointing after everyone's hard work, but shipping a secure, stable product is worth the extra three weeks. Updated milestones will be shared at tomorrow's stand-up.

Thank you all for your continued dedication and effort.
