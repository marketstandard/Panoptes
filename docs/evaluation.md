# Evaluation methodology

Panoptes evaluation follows the frozen measurement protocol in [`docs/research-protocol.md`](research-protocol.md) (`research/protocol.json`, registered 2026-08-13). Attribution is treated as a measurement problem: the evidential meaning of a detector score depends on population, prevalence, domain, manipulation history, provenance, and calibration cohort.

## Research questions

1. **RQ1 Calibration.** Does a reported P(AI participation) match empirical frequency under a declared population?
2. **RQ2 Distribution shift.** How much does attribution degrade under domain, generator, prompt, time, or editing shift?
3. **RQ3 Evidence aggregation.** Do calibration, Bayesian aggregation, uncertainty, and abstention improve trustworthiness over raw scores?
4. **RQ4 Reproducibility.** Can the claim be verified from committed datasets, artifacts, manifests, and results?

H1–H6 in `research/hypotheses.json` remain the stylometry battery. They are supporting diagnostics, not the primary claims.

## Tasks (kept distinct)

| Task | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Human-only | No AI generation or substantive AI rewriting. |
| AI-generated | Majority or entirety generated by an AI system. |
| AI-assisted | Human-authored, with substantial AI generation or modification. |
| Unknown | Evidence is insufficient for a supported attribution. |

Outputs: **P(AI participation)**, **P(AI generation)**, and **source-family attribution** (with unknown). Watermark tests are a separate subsystem.

## Split discipline

Hard rule: **train → calibration → test**. Never fit or tune calibration on the final test set.

- Splits are group-disjoint on prompt (and on author/source when those fields exist).
- Default when `n_groups < 30`: nested grouped cross-validation.
- Otherwise: 60/20/20 group holdout.
- Official publisher splits are audited for leakage and unused when they leak groups.
- Unknown-generator experiments train on known families and test held-out families as open-set rejection.

Run with:

```bash
python -m bench measure --data corpus
```

## Metrics

Primary:

- AUROC, AUPRC;
- TPR at 0.1%, 1%, and 5% FPR;
- Brier score, ECE, calibration slope and intercept;
- selective risk at coverage 100%, 95%, 90%, 80%, 70%, 50%;
- worst-group performance;
- train-domain × test-domain transport (AUROC, Brier, ECE, slope);
- macro-F1 and unknown-rejection AUROC for attribution.

Accuracy at 0.5 is not a primary scientific metric. ECE is a diagnostic of the probability map; it does not discount the posterior.

## Evidence model

Calibrated probability \(p\) and cohort prevalence \(\pi\) yield

\[
\mathrm{LR}=\frac{p}{1-p}\cdot\frac{1-\pi}{\pi},\qquad O_1=O_0\times\mathrm{LR}.
\]

Dependence models compared: naive sum of segment log-LRs, correlation-aware shrinkage, document-level evidence. A non-rejected Durbin–Watson test is not treated as proof of independence.

Priors for sensitivity: 0.1%, 0.5%, 1%, 5%, 10%, 25%, 50%, 75%.

## Current status

The shipped calibration artifact (`backend/artifacts/baseline-calibration.json`) is fitted on the verified reference corpus — 104 hash-verified records (96 AI outputs across 6 model families, 8 human controls) — via isotonic regression with grouped cross-validation, corpus-fitted source-family geometry, and conformal thresholds. The methodology report (`backend/artifacts/methodology-report.json`) records the VIF feature screening, pre-registered hypothesis tests (H1–H6) with Benjamini–Hochberg q-values, and the econometric specification battery, per cohort (`cohorts.corpus` and `cohorts.defactify`). The synthetic development artifact remains reproducible via `python research/calibration.py --synthetic` for pipeline testing.

Honest statistical caveats: at n=104 the corpus supports calibration and tier-0/tier-1 modeling, but hypothesis tests are underpowered for small effects (≈23% power for a d=0.5 two-group difference), and on that corpus the neural tier (Panoptes-v0) is gated accordingly. Growing the corpus through community baseline submissions directly increases what the methodology can conclude. The measurement protocol still runs on this corpus as a leakage-safe pilot (`python -m bench measure`).

### Defactify external validation (n=71,666)

The release-quality external evaluation now exists: the bench runs on the Defactify_Text_Dataset (Roy et al. 2026, arXiv:2510.22874), fetched and hygiene-filtered by `research/fetch_defactify.py` (73,193 raw → 71,666 clean: 412 API-error artifacts, 73 exact duplicates, and 957 sub-50-token texts dropped). Raw text stays local and gitignored; a signed pointer manifest lives at `datasets/manifests/defactify-text.json`.

- **Leakage audit.** TF-IDF story-group reconstruction (cosine threshold 0.45) finds 61,006 groups; 11.5% of the official test split shares a story with official train. All reported numbers use story-grouped GroupKFold, not the official splits.
- **Detection (out-of-fold).** Logistic tier-0 AUROC 0.989 (95% CI 0.988–0.990, ECE 0.006); GBM tier-1 AUROC 0.999 (ECE 0.005); Panoptes-v0 with the sequence branch admitted AUROC 0.998 (ECE 0.019). The dataset authors' baselines score 53–58% accuracy.
- **Domain shift (central result).** The shipped runtime (heuristic + corpus-fitted calibration, zero-shot on Defactify) attains AUROC 0.648 (95% CI 0.643–0.652, ECE 0.029). The Defactify-trained ensemble transported back to the project corpus attains AUROC 0.471. That collapse is the scientific center of the study, not a caveat.
- **Hypotheses at full power.** The pre-registered H1–H6 battery re-runs unchanged: H1–H5 nulls rejected (q ≤ 0.0024), H6 (segment autocorrelation) not rejected (q = 1.0) — and is not interpreted as proof of independence.
- **Exploratory attribution.** Seven-class source attribution (human + six LLM families) with multinomial logistic and a K=7 Dirichlet Panoptes-v0 variant; per-family F1 against the authors' 5–9% band in `backend/artifacts/cards/attribution-defactify.json`.
- **Dual calibration.** A second signed artifact (`backend/artifacts/defactify-calibration.json`) fits the isotonic map, reliability bins, conformal threshold, and seven-family Mahalanobis geometry on Defactify. It is opt-in: `PANOPTES_CALIBRATION_BUNDLE=defactify-calibration.json` (allowlist-validated, falls back to the corpus-fitted default).
